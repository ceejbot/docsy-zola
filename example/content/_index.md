This site is the theme's demo and its documentation at once. It is built from
the `example/` directory of the [docsy-zola repository](https://github.com/ceejbot/docsy-zola)
with a stylesheet that is nothing but the theme import, so the colours, type
and spacing you see here are docsy's defaults.

Look around: the left column is the site tree, the right column is this page's
table of contents, the search box works offline, and the toggle in the navbar
switches between light, dark and automatic colour modes.
